Brightfield illumination of a human thyroid at x10 and x100 magnification using oil immersion, stained with H&E. Colloid scalloping is visible in the x100 magnification inset. This occurs when active cells resorb thyroglobulin-containing colloid to break it down into T3 and T4 for secretion into the blood.
