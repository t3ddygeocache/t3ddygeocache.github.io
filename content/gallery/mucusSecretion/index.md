Brightfield illumination of ciliated epithelium of a human trachea at x100 magnification using oil immersion. With H&E staining, the mucin droplets are distinct, shown in varying stages of secretion. Mucin traps foreign particles, dust and pathogens in the airways for clearance, as well as maintaining moisture.
