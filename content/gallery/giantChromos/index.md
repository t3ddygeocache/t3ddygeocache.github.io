Chironomus larva salivary glands
Polytene chromosomes are chromosomes which undergo multiple rounds of replication without separating, resulting in strands which stay fused together. As a result, areas of heterochromatin - compacted chromatin - and euchromatin - loose chromatin being used for active transcription - are visible.

This sample is from a section of the salivary glands of a Chironomus larva, stained with acetocarmine and observed at x100 magnification with oil immersion.
