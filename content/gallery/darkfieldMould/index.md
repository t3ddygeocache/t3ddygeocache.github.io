Darkfield illumination of an unkown mould at 10x magnification, with no staining. The darkfield illumination demonstrates the distinct septate hyphae, as well as the scattered conidia. These characteristics combined with the substrate being coffee indicate it could be Aspergillus or Penicillium, although no intact conidiophore could be found to distinguish them.
