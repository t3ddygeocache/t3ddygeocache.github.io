Oblique illumination of a human blood sample mixed with baker's yeast, at x40 magnification. A phagocyte attempts to phagocytose a congregation of three yeast cells, but is unable to engulf them, demonstrating frustrated phagocytosis. This results in the recruitment of another phagocyte, although this is also unable to engulf the yeast, likely due to the pressure from the coverslip. The phagocytes eventually release the yeast cells and move on. Throughout the video, the degradation of already-engulfed yeast cells is visible in the phagocyte, with two cells merging as they degrade.
